# Business Overview

Restaurant Name:
AFC – Absolutely Fried Chicken

Business Type:
Quick Service Restaurant (QSR)

Current Branches:
6 branches

Future Goal:
Expand to 25+ franchise branches across multiple cities.

---

# Current Operations

We currently do not have any dedicated POS software.

Most operations are handled manually.

Billing is done manually using printed bill books or basic computer invoices.

Inventory is maintained in Excel sheets and sometimes manually in notebooks.

Each branch manages its own stock independently.

There is no centralized reporting.

---

# Current Problems

- Billing takes too much time during rush hours.
- Difficult to know daily sales from all branches.
- Inventory wastage is high.
- Raw materials are not tracked properly.
- No centralized dashboard.
- Difficult to compare branch performance.
- Reports are created manually.
- Difficult to identify bestselling products.
- No customer purchase history.
- Hard to monitor staff activities.
